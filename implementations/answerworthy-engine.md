## Current public claim

The Engine is intended to collect evidence, evaluate the declared conditions, create and maintain an `answerworthy.md` action queue, respect human authority boundaries, and recheck resulting state.

The target is full condition coverage. That target is a design declaration until a public implementation and retained assessment evidence establish the actual depth of each condition.

## Verification requirement

Before this profile reports a verified score, a reproducible assessment should retain:

- the Standard version;
- the Engine version or commit;
- the test business/fixture and declared permissions;
- outputs for all eight conditions;
- evidence for each assigned depth;
- re-verification behaviour after at least one material change;
- any conditions or surfaces the Engine could not reach.

Because the steward maintains both the Standard and this reference implementation, the public evidence should be strong enough for an independent reviewer to reproduce or challenge the mapping.
