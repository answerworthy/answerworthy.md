# Does your commuter bike need a service or a repair?

Choose the appointment that matches what your bicycle needs, so we can plan the right work when you drop it off at Acme Cycle Workshop in Manchester.

## Book a commuter service for routine attention

Our **£60 commuter service** includes a safety check, brake and gear adjustment, chain lubrication, and a check of tyre condition and pressure on a conventional bicycle.

The price covers this labour. If replacement parts are needed, we will tell you the price and ask for your approval before fitting them.

## Choose a repair assessment for a specific problem

Persistent slipping gears, repeated punctures, damage, or a fault you cannot identify call for a repair assessment. Tell us what happens and when: for example, whether a noise occurs while pedalling or a gear slips under load. Repairs are quoted after assessment and are not automatically included in the £60 service.

You can check tyre pressure against the range marked on the tyre before booking and note any symptoms. These checks help you describe the problem; they do not confirm the bicycle is safe to ride. If the brakes are unreliable or the bicycle appears damaged, do not ride it to us.

## Check these limits before you book

- We do not repair e-bike motors or batteries. Take those faults to an e-bike specialist.
- Appointments are weekday drop-off times. Same-day completion is not guaranteed.
- If you need your bicycle back by a particular time, tell us so we can discuss whether the appointment suits you.

**Choose your appointment**

On Acme's booking page, select **Commuter service** for routine work or **Repair assessment** for a particular fault. Have your bicycle type, symptoms if any, and preferred weekday ready.
