# Acme Cycle Workshop — example source material

Acme is fictional. This complete fixture supplies the source text for the worked example; no website, customer quotation, research finding, or commercial result has been retrieved. F1–F3 stand for existing public business pages within the scenario. F4 is a supplied owner brief, included here so the example is portable. Page paths identify intended business pages inside the scenario, not live sources to fetch.

## F1 — Existing homepage (`/`)

**Keep your commute rolling.**

Acme Cycle Workshop services and repairs conventional bicycles in Manchester. Weekday appointments, clear service terms, and approval before extra parts are fitted.

See our commuter service (F2) and booking details (F3).

## F2 — Existing service page (`/services/commuter`)

### Commuter service — £60 labour

For a conventional bicycle that needs routine attention, our commuter service includes a safety check, brake and gear adjustment, chain lubrication, and a check of tyre condition and pressure.

The £60 price covers this labour. Replacement parts cost extra. We contact you with the price and obtain your approval before fitting them.

If the bicycle has persistent slipping gears, repeated punctures, damage, or a fault you cannot identify, choose a repair assessment and describe the problem. Assessment findings determine the proposed repair and quote; those repairs are not automatically included in the £60 service.

We do not repair e-bike motors or batteries. For these faults, contact an e-bike specialist.

Book a weekday appointment and leave your bicycle with us. Check the booking details before choosing a time.

## F3 — Existing booking page (`/book`)

Choose **Commuter service** for the routine work listed on the service page, or **Repair assessment** for a specific fault, persistent problem, or damage. The booking form asks for your bicycle type, the problem if any, and your preferred weekday appointment.

Before booking, you can check tyre pressure against the range marked on the tyre and note when any noise or slipping occurs. These checks help describe the problem; they do not establish that a bicycle is safe to ride. If the brakes do not work reliably or the bicycle appears damaged, do not ride it to the workshop.

An appointment is a drop-off time, not a promise of same-day completion. Tell us if you need the bicycle back by a particular time so we can discuss whether the appointment suits you. Repairs and extra parts require an agreed quote.
