# Changelog

Material changes to the Answerworthy Standard are recorded here. Working drafts are not normative releases until merged into the canonical organisation-owned repository and released there.

## 1.1-draft — unreleased

This draft develops the original eight-condition model without changing its central purpose: making a business understandable, verifiable and defensibly choosable by AI for the right buyer.

### Added

- explicit definition of **choosable** as a defensible fit for a particular buyer over relevant alternatives;
- falsifiable buyer **Outcomes** as the goal of an implementation;
- explicit full or partial condition coverage;
- stable Outcome IDs and Action-to-Outcome links;
- decision-fit and rationale support fields for Observed results;
- materiality requirement for Actions, so detected imperfections do not automatically become work;
- re-observation of affected buyer Outcomes after implementation changes;
- explicit governance, versioning and normative/non-normative boundaries;
- implementation-registry methodology separating reference implementations, declared implementations and independently mapped products;
- evidence levels separating declared, demonstrated and verified implementation capability.

### Changed

- `Understood` now includes fit, trade-offs, relevant alternatives and honest exclusions rather than only offer description;
- `Answering` now requires decision-useful distinctions rather than extractable answers alone;
- `Observed` acts as the integration test for whether the right buyer, choice and supporting rationale survived the full chain;
- the measurement Rule now states that scores, counts and positions are evidence, not objectives, and must not become universal grades;
- conformance now includes an `actionable` state for plans whose active Actions advance Outcomes and define completion and verification.

### Clarified

- partial use of the Standard is valid when coverage is declared;
- reference implementations and commercial services do not become normative merely because they are maintained by the steward;
- product names, domains and branding do not establish adoption, affiliation or conformance;
- implementation assessments may summarise workflow depth, but any aggregate must remain subordinate to the visible condition matrix and evidence basis.

## 1.0 — initial public specification — 2026-08-06

- defined the eight conditions: Reachable, Identified, Understood, Answering, Evidenced, Corroborated, Distributed and Observed;
- defined the six-section `answerworthy.md` working file;
- established evidence states and the honesty Rules;
- established human boundaries for business claims and third-party communication;
- defined declared, evidenced and maintained file conformance;
- licensed the Standard text under CC BY 4.0 and the template/example under CC0.
